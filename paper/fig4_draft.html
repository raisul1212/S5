Historical reference only. Mean test accuracy on LRA-ListOps (n = 8 seeds) plotted against total digital chip energy per inference under two chip topology choices. Config 4 (Mambino, 106 K params) sits alone on the frontier in both topologies — but this ordering is trivially driven by MAC count and does not reflect a real dataflow-based chip PPAC.

Fig 4. Accuracy vs digital chip energy per inference. Each point is one architectural configuration; y-axis is the n = 8 mean test-at-peak-validation accuracy from Fig 3, x-axis is total chip energy per LRA-ListOps (L = 2048) inference from Accelergy digital PPAC. The dashed Pareto frontier passes through points that are not dominated on either axis — Config 4 (Mambino, 106 K) is the frontier's minimum-energy vertex in both topologies. Corner 3′ (Mambino, 188 K) is the frontier's high-accuracy vertex. The 106 K and 188 K parameter bands are shaded for visual grouping.
