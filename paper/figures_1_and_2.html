Figure 1

Mambino layer architecture: main state-space model, forward-only predictor branch, additive predictive-coding feedback, and two output-activation variants

main SSM path predictor branch PC surprise + W_ε feedback GELU (variant A) gated output (variant B)

Figure 1. One Mambino layer. Input x[t] fans out to three destinations: the main SSM's input projection B̄·x, the surprise subtraction ε = x − pred, and a forward-only predictor SSM with independent parameters Λ_s, B_s, C_s. The surprise ε[t] is projected through the additive feedback matrix W̄_ε and summed with B̄·x to form the main scan's input Bu[t]. The main SSM runs a bidirectional associative scan; forward and backward state trajectories are concatenated along local_P before the output projection C̃. The output activation splits into two variants: variant A is a pointwise GELU (no gate matrices — used by Configs 4 & 5); variant B is a half_glu2 gate with rank r — full-rank in Corner 1 (r = H) and low-rank in Corner 3′ (r = 40). Under the Pure S5 baseline (Configs 5 and Corner 1), the entire predictor branch and W̄_ε feedback are removed and Bu = B̄·x only. Mambino's isolated architectural contribution is exactly the predictor branch and the W̄_ε feedback loop; the activation choice is orthogonal.

Figure 2

Chip block topologies compared: pipelined vs sequential-layer streaming inference at 22 nm

SRAM · on-chip memory digital compute · MAC / regfile SSM layer stage

Figure 2. Two chip topologies that differ only in activation-SRAM depth and layer-pipeline width. The pipelined chip (left) keeps a 4.2 MB activation SRAM buffering the forward + backward pass streams of all eight Mambino layers concurrently and pipelines them at one timestep per stage per cycle, maximising throughput. The sequential chip (right) collapses to a single reused layer stage with a 512 KB activation SRAM buffering one layer at a time, cutting die area by ~4× and per-inference energy by ~3× at the cost of ~8× longer latency. State SRAM sizing follows the streaming formula local_P × L × n_layers × 1 under the pipelined topology and local_P × L × 1 × 1 under the sequential topology (master doc §11). Weight SRAM, MAC array, and register file are identical across topologies. All PPAC totals are Accelergy 0.4 + CACTI + NeuroSim at 22 nm INT8, 1 GHz.
