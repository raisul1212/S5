Accuracy on LRA-ListOps at two matched-parameter budgets

Test accuracy at peak validation epoch, mean ± SEM over n = 8 matched seeds {6554595, 42, 12345, 271828, 314159, 1, 2, 3}. Individual seed dots overlaid. Paired-t significance annotations between pairs.

Fig 3. Test accuracy at peak validation on LRA-ListOps (L = 2048, 10-class, n = 8 matched seeds) for both parameter budgets. Bars show the mean; whiskers show ±SEM (standard error of the mean, n = 8); dots show individual seed accuracies. Paired-t brackets: n.s. is not significant at α = 0.05 two-tailed; * is p < 0.05, ** is p < 0.01, *** is p < 0.001. The y-axis is clipped to make within-band differences visible; the ⩘ break marker indicates the clip.
